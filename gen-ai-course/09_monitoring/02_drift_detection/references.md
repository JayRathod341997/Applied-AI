- **Evidently AI — Data & ML Drift** — open-source library and docs covering PSI, KS, Wasserstein, and embedding-drift detection with worked examples: https://docs.evidentlyai.com/
- **Evidently AI — Which test to use for drift detection** — a practical guide to choosing between PSI, KS, chi-square, and others: https://www.evidentlyai.com/blog/data-drift-detection-large-datasets

- **Gama et al. — A Survey on Concept Drift Adaptation** — the canonical academic survey of drift types and adaptation strategies (ACM Computing Surveys, 2014): https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/2523813
- **Amazon SageMaker Model Monitor** — managed data-quality and drift monitoring for deployed models: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/sagemaker/latest/dg/model-monitor.html
- **Google Vertex AI — Model Monitoring** — feature-skew and feature-drift detection on GCP: https://cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/docs/model-monitoring/overview
- **Arize — Embedding Drift Monitoring** — detecting and visualising drift in embedding space for LLM/NLP systems: https://docs.arize.com/arize/machine-learning/how-to-ml/drift
- **Fiddler AI — Drift Detection** — production drift monitoring and explainability: https://docs.fiddler.ai/
- **Page–Hinkley & change-point detection (scikit-multiflow)** — streaming drift detectors for online monitoring: https://scikit-multiflow.readthedocs.io/
