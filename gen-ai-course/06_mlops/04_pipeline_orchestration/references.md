# References

- **Apache Airflow Documentation** — DAGs, operators, scheduling, sensors, retries, and backfills in the most widely used orchestrator: https://airflow.apache.org/docs/
- **Airflow — Core Concepts (DAGs)** — how DAGs, tasks, and dependencies are defined and ordered: https://airflow.apache.org/docs/apache-airflow/stable/core-concepts/dags.html
- **Prefect Documentation** — Pythonic flows and tasks, dynamic DAGs, retries, schedules, and event-driven automations: https://docs.prefect.io/
- **Dagster Documentation** — software-defined assets, the asset graph, lineage, schedules, sensors, and auto-materialize: https://docs.dagster.io/
- **Kubeflow Pipelines** — building and orchestrating portable, containerized ML pipelines on Kubernetes: https://www.kubeflow.org/docs/components/pipelines/
- **Topological Sorting (Wikipedia)** — formal definition and properties of topological order on a DAG: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topological_sorting
- **Kahn's Algorithm (GeeksforGeeks)** — step-by-step in-degree-based topological sort with cycle detection: https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/topological-sorting-indegree-based-solution/
- **Google Cloud — MLOps: Continuous delivery and automation pipelines in ML** — the canonical MLOps maturity model covering training/serving pipelines and retraining triggers: https://cloud.google.com/architecture/mlops-continuous-delivery-and-automation-pipelines-in-machine-learning
- **Martin Fowler — Continuous Delivery for Machine Learning (CD4ML)** — pipelines, automation, and reproducibility for ML systems: https://martinfowler.com/articles/cd4ml.html
- **Evidently AI — Data & Concept Drift** — practical guide to drift metrics (PSI, etc.) that power drift-based retraining triggers: https://www.evidentlyai.com/ml-in-production/data-drift
- **crontab.guru** — interactive reference for writing and reading cron schedule expressions: https://crontab.guru/
- **Idempotency (Wikipedia)** — the formal notion of idempotent operations that underpins safe retries and backfills: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idempotence
